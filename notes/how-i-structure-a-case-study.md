# How I structure every case study on this site

Every project page here follows the same outline. I chose it so that a reader who has two minutes can find the question, the answer and the evidence without hunting for them.

## The outline

1. **Business problem.** Who needs to decide something, and what are they deciding?
2. **Dataset.** Where the data came from, with a link, so anyone can check my work.
3. **Approach.** The steps I took, in order.
4. **Tools.** What I used for each step.
5. **Dashboard or visual.** The thing a stakeholder would actually look at.
6. **Key insights.** What the data showed, each with the number behind it.
7. **Recommendations.** What I would suggest doing about it.
8. **What I'd improve next.** The limits of the analysis and what I would add with more time.
9. **Links.** The code and the live dashboard.

## Why it starts with a problem

It is tempting to open with the most impressive chart. I start with the business problem because a chart without a question is decoration. If I cannot say who would use the analysis and what they would do differently, I am not ready to build the dashboard.

## What "coming soon" means

Some pages on this site still show sections marked as coming soon. That is deliberate. I would rather show an unfinished project honestly than fill the gap with numbers I have not earned yet.
